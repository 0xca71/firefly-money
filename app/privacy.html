Privacy

Money is a self-hosted dashboard for your own Firefly III. There is no Money company server, no account with us and no tracking.

What stays on your server

  • Your transactions, accounts and budgets live in your Firefly III. Money only passes them to your browser.
  • Your Firefly token stays on the server and is never sent to the browser.

What is stored in your browser

  • One sign-in cookie (HttpOnly, signed by the server), only so you stay signed in. It is not used for tracking.
  • Preferences such as theme and layout, in local storage on your device. Some are also saved to your own Firefly.

What leaves your server

  • Payee logos are fetched by your server from DuckDuckGo's icon service. They are cached, and your browser never contacts it.
  • Map pictures come from OpenStreetMap, and optional store lookups from Overpass, also through your server.
  • No analytics, advertising or third-party scripts run in the app.
