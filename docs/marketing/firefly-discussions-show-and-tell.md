Title: Money: a phone-first front end for Firefly III (open source, live demo)

I built a small web front end that sits next to your Firefly III and talks to it through the API. It doesn't change Firefly or add a database.

What it does:
- Quick add in plain words: `coffee 5.40 amex yesterday`
- Apple Pay auto-logging from an iPhone Shortcut
- Safe-to-spend until payday, forecast with what-ifs, bills calendar, monthly recap
- Installable on phones, 12 themes
- One nginx container; your token stays on the server

Live demo (fake data, no sign-in): https://muffy0906.github.io/firefly-money/
Repo: https://github.com/Muffy0906/firefly-money

Feedback welcome, especially from people with big or unusual Firefly setups.
